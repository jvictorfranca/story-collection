Title: Jack and the Beanstalk
Language: en

Once upon a time, a poor boy named Jack lived with his mother in a small cottage. They had very little money, and their only possession of value was an old cow.

One morning, Jack's mother told him that they had to sell the cow so they could buy food.

Jack took the cow to the market. On the way, he met a strange old man who offered him five magical beans in exchange for the cow.

Jack thought the beans were wonderful and agreed to the trade.

When he returned home, his mother was furious.

"You traded our cow for a handful of beans!" she cried.

She threw the beans out of the window and sent Jack to bed without supper.

During the night, something magical happened. The beans grew into a gigantic beanstalk that reached high into the clouds.

When Jack woke up and saw it, he decided to climb.

He climbed higher and higher until he reached a strange land above the clouds. There stood a huge castle.

Jack entered the castle and met a giant's wife.

He was hungry, so she gave him some bread and milk. But before he could finish, the giant returned.

"Fee-fi-fo-fum! I smell the blood of an English boy!"

Jack quickly hid inside a cupboard.

The giant searched the room but could not find him. Then he sat down and counted his treasures.

After the giant fell asleep, Jack saw a golden harp and a hen that laid golden eggs.

Jack quietly took the hen and the harp and ran away.

The harp cried out, waking the giant.

Jack rushed toward the beanstalk with the giant close behind him.

He climbed down as quickly as he could.

When Jack reached the ground, he called to his mother for an axe.

Together, they chopped down the enormous beanstalk.

The giant fell from the clouds and was never seen again.

The golden hen provided Jack and his mother with enough money to live comfortably, and from that day forward they never went hungry again.
