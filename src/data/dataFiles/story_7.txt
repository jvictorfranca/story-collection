Title: The Three Little Pigs
Language: en

Once upon a time, three little pigs left their mother's home to build houses of their own.

The first pig built his house from straw because it was quick and easy.

The second pig built his house from sticks.

The third pig worked much harder and built his house from bricks.

One day, a hungry wolf came walking through the forest.

He saw the first pig's straw house and knocked on the door.

"Little pig, little pig, let me come in!"

"No, no, no!" said the pig.

"Then I will blow your house down!"

The wolf took a deep breath and blew with all his strength.

The straw house fell apart, and the little pig ran to his brother's house.

The wolf followed them.

He knocked on the house made of sticks.

"Little pigs, little pigs, let me come in!"

"No, no, no!"

"Then I will blow your house down!"

The wolf blew as hard as he could.

The house of sticks fell apart.

The two pigs ran to their brother's brick house.

The wolf followed them there.

He knocked on the door.

"Little pigs, let me come in!"

"No!" they shouted.

The wolf blew and blew, but the brick house did not move.

He tried again and again, but the house remained strong.

Finally, the wolf became tired.

He noticed the chimney and decided to climb down it.

But the three pigs had prepared a large pot of hot water beneath the fireplace.

The wolf landed beside the pot, became frightened, and quickly climbed back up the chimney.

He ran away into the forest and never returned.

The three pigs learned that careful work and strong foundations can protect us when trouble comes.
