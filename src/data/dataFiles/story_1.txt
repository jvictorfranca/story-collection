Title: Goldilocks and the Three Bears
Language: en

Once upon a time, there were three bears who lived together in a little house in the forest. There was a great big Bear, a middle-sized Bear, and a little Bear.

One morning, the three bears made some porridge for breakfast. They put the porridge into three bowls, but it was too hot to eat, so they decided to take a walk while it cooled.

Not far from the bears' house lived a little girl named Goldilocks. One day, while walking through the forest, she came upon the bears' house.

Goldilocks knocked on the door, but nobody answered. She knocked again, but the house remained quiet. Curious, she opened the door and went inside.

On the table she saw three bowls of porridge.

Goldilocks tasted the porridge in the great big bowl.

"This porridge is too hot!" she said.

Then she tasted the porridge in the middle-sized bowl.

"This porridge is too cold!"

Finally, she tasted the porridge in the little bowl.

"This porridge is just right!"

She ate it all.

After eating, Goldilocks saw three chairs near the fireplace. She sat in the great big chair, but it was too hard. She tried the middle-sized chair, but it was too soft.

Then she sat in the little chair.

"This chair is just right!" she said.

But the little chair broke beneath her, and Goldilocks fell onto the floor.

Goldilocks was tired, so she went upstairs and found three beds.

The great big bed was too hard. The middle-sized bed was too soft. But the little bed was just right.

Goldilocks climbed into the little bed and quickly fell asleep.

Soon afterward, the three bears returned from their walk.

The great big Bear looked at his bowl.

"Someone has been eating my porridge," he said.

The middle-sized Bear looked at her bowl.

"Someone has been eating my porridge too."

The little Bear looked at his bowl.

"Someone has eaten all my porridge!"

Then they noticed their chairs.

"Someone has been sitting in my chair," said the great big Bear.

"Someone has been sitting in my chair too," said the middle-sized Bear.

"Someone has been sitting in my chair, and it is broken!" cried the little Bear.

The three bears went upstairs.

The great big Bear looked at his bed.

"Someone has been sleeping in my bed."

The middle-sized Bear looked at her bed.

"Someone has been sleeping in my bed too."

Then the little Bear looked at his bed.

"Someone is sleeping in my bed!"

Goldilocks woke up and saw the three bears standing beside her.

She jumped out of bed, ran downstairs, opened the door, and hurried all the way home.

From that day on, Goldilocks remembered that it was never right to enter someone else's house or use their things without permission.
