Title: Little Red Riding Hood
Language: en

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who always wore a red hood made for her by her grandmother. Because of this, everyone called her Little Red Riding Hood.

One morning, her mother gave her a basket of food.

"Take this to your grandmother," she said. "She is feeling ill. Stay on the path and do not stop along the way."

Little Red Riding Hood promised to be careful and walked into the forest.

Along the path, she met a wolf.

"Where are you going?" asked the wolf.

"I am going to visit my grandmother," Little Red Riding Hood replied.

The wolf asked where her grandmother lived, and Little Red Riding Hood told him.

The wolf secretly hurried ahead while the girl stopped to admire flowers along the path.

The wolf reached the grandmother's house first. He knocked on the door and entered.

He disguised himself as the grandmother and climbed into her bed.

When Little Red Riding Hood arrived, she entered the room.

"Grandmother, your ears are so big!"

"So I can hear you better," said the wolf.

"Your eyes are so big!"

"So I can see you better."

"Your teeth are so big!"

"So I can eat you better!"

The wolf jumped out of bed.

Little Red Riding Hood cried for help.

A woodcutter who was working nearby heard her voice and rushed into the house. He frightened the wolf away, and the wolf ran deep into the forest.

Little Red Riding Hood hugged her grandmother.

"I am sorry I did not follow the path and listened to a stranger," she said.

From then on, she remembered her mother's warning and never wandered away from the path without permission.
