Title: The Tortoise and the Hare
Language: en

Once upon a time, a hare was very proud of how quickly he could run.

He often laughed at the tortoise because the tortoise walked so slowly.

"Why do you move so slowly?" the hare asked. "You could never win a race against me."

The tortoise calmly replied, "Perhaps I could."

The hare laughed.

"Then let us have a race!"

The other animals agreed to watch.

The race began.

The hare ran so quickly that he soon disappeared from sight.

The tortoise continued walking slowly, one step after another.

After running a long distance, the hare looked back and saw no sign of the tortoise.

"I have plenty of time," he thought.

He found a shady tree and decided to rest.

Soon the hare fell asleep.

Meanwhile, the tortoise continued walking.

He did not stop. He did not hurry. He simply kept moving toward the finish line.

After a long time, the tortoise passed the sleeping hare.

Eventually, the hare woke up.

He saw that the tortoise was almost at the finish line and ran as fast as he could.

But it was too late.

The tortoise crossed the finish line first.

The animals cheered.

The hare was embarrassed.

He had been so confident in his speed that he forgot that patience and persistence were important too.

The tortoise smiled and said, "Slow and steady can win the race."
